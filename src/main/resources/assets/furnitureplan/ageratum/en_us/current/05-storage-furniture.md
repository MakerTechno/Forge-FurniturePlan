# Storage Furniture

## Cupboards

A cupboard has three independent drawers with 9, 9, and 12 slots. Click the matching region on the front to open a drawer. Its screen supports the player inventory, hotbar, and shift-click transfers.

## Cabinets

A cabinet is one 36-slot container using a four-row vanilla container screen. Each body material has one disguised material frame and sixteen colored frame variants.

## Shared Rules

Both furniture types save inventories and drop their contents when broken. They must be operated from the front and cannot open when being obstructed that side. Automation can currently access the complete container without directional input or output rules.
