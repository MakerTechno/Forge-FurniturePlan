# Seating, Tables, and Columns

## Chairs and Benches

Use an empty hand on a chair or bench to sit. An invisible entity provides the seat, and each piece supports one rider at a time. Removing the furniture also removes its seat entity.

## Tables

Horizontally adjacent tables automatically switch edge, leg, and panel shapes. Connections check the table block type rather than material, so tables made from different materials may still connect.

## Columns

Columns, carved columns, and lighted columns switch between base, middle, and top shapes according to vertical neighbors. Different materials and column styles may participate in the same connection. Lighted columns always emit light level 15 and currently cannot be switched off.
