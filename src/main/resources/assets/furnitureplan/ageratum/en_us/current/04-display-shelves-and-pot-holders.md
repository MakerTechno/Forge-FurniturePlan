# Display Shelves and Pot Holders

## Wall Shelves

A wall shelf starts with three display slots. Use another matching shelf on it to add a second level and increase the total to six. Use an item on an empty slot to place one item, use an empty hand to rotate it, and sneak-use with an empty hand to remove it. When the lower level is empty, another removal action dismantles the second level and returns the shelf item.

## Moon Shelves

A moon shelf is a fixed 3×3 multiblock structure requiring nine replaceable spaces. It provides 22 display slots. Placement, rotation, and removal follow the wall shelf controls. Displayed items drop when the structure is broken.

## Pot Holders

Pot holders exist in sixteen colors, occupy two blocks vertically, and provide three planting positions. First use a vanilla flower pot on an empty position, then add a plant accepted by vanilla flower pots. Using an empty hand removes the plant and pot in sequence. Contents are also returned when the holder is broken.
