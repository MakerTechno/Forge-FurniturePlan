# Compatibility, Debugging, and Limitations

## Known Limitations

- There are no survival recipes and no registered furniture graver item.
- The stove, water dispenser, and standalone water bottle are ordinary placeholder blocks without their intended processing or water behavior.
- Pots and plates have no fuel, progress, or recipe processing system.
- Display shelves have no GUI and require accurate slot targeting.
- Moon shelves require the full 3×3 space, and lighted columns cannot be switched off.
- There is no player-facing configuration system.

## Troubleshooting

If the guidebook does not open, confirm that Ageratum is loaded on the client and that `/ageratum "furnitureplan" "index"` is available. For furniture interaction problems, verify the clicked face, sneaking state, held item, structure space, and support blocks described in the relevant chapter.

This documentation describes the current Minecraft 26.1.2 workspace and is not a guarantee for older releases.
