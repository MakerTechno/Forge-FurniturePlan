# Tableware, Lighting, and Novelty Items

## Tableware

A plate can combine with cutlery and a drinking glass and can hold one item with a food component. With cutlery present, food on the plate, and a hungry player, using an empty hand eats the food. Sneak-use with an empty hand removes components step by step.

The drinking glass block accepts potions, can be drunk from with an empty hand, and can return its potion to a glass bottle. Use an empty glass item on a water source to fill it. An iron pot holds one food item. The iron pot item also acts as a melee weapon with strong knockback and random negative effects.

## Lighting

Use an empty hand to toggle a table lamp. Use flint and steel to toggle either hanging lantern style. They emit light level 15 while lit. Lighted columns remain permanently lit.

## Novelty Items

The Grass item can be placed and eaten. Eating it grants strong Darkness and Speed effects. It can occupy equipment slots but has no dedicated worn model or additional equipment behavior.
