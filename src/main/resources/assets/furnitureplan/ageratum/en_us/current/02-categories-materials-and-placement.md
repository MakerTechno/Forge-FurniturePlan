# Categories, Materials, and Placement

## Categories

- Sitting: chairs and benches.
- Surfacing: tables, pot holders, wall shelves, and moon shelves.
- Decorating: columns, carved columns, and lighted columns.
- Storage: three-drawer cupboards and four-row cabinets.
- Miscellaneous: lanterns, table lamps, iron pots, plates, drinking glasses, cutlery, and grass.

## Material Sets

Furniture covers many vanilla appearances, including wood, stone, bricks, Nether materials, copper, metal blocks, ice, snow, and all sixteen concrete colors. Most furniture inherits hardness, sounds, and mining tags from its base block. The sixteen color sets also include pot holders. Cabinets are available with disguised material frames or sixteen colored frames.

## Placement

Furniture with a front usually faces the placer. Many pieces support waterlogging. Small tableware and lights require support above or below and are removed when that support disappears. Moon shelves and pot holders are multiblock structures and require their full space before placement.

Unwaxed cut copper furniture randomly oxidizes using vanilla copper behavior. Waxed sets keep a fixed appearance.
